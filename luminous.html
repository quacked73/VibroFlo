Test Your Hardware
Some light-and-sound machines — like the MindPlace Kasina — read tones hidden above normal hearing range to flash their LEDs in sync with audio. AudioStrobe uses one tone for brightness only; SpectraStrobe uses four for independent color. Use this to confirm your specific device and cables actually reproduce whichever one you're testing.
