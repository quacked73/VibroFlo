Test Your Hardware
Some light-and-sound machines — like the MindPlace Kasina — read a special tone hidden above normal hearing range (around 19.2kHz) to flash their LEDs in sync with audio. This is called AudioStrobe. Use this to test your connected hardware directly, separate from a real session — useful for confirming your specific device and cables actually reproduce this signal before relying on it live. Almost nobody can actually hear this tone — but some phone speakers and cheap headphones don't reproduce frequencies this high cleanly, which is exactly what this is for figuring out.
