What this is
Some light-and-sound machines — like the MindPlace Kasina — read a special tone hidden above normal hearing range (around 19.2kHz) to flash their LEDs in sync with audio. This is called AudioStrobe. This page generates that signal on its own, separate from a regular session, so it can be tested against real hardware before it's wired into the tone engines. Almost nobody can actually hear this tone — but some phone speakers and cheap headphones don't reproduce frequencies this high cleanly, which is exactly what this page is for figuring out.
